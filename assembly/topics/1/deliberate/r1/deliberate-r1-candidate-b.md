# Candidate B - Compare what you Buy

**This candidate prioritizes the same engineer first, then raises someone comparing across providers to second place, which neither of the others does. That asks more of every figure, because a number has to hold up against one produced by a different organisation.**

### 1st "Did this release, configuration, or architectural change reduce energy or carbon for the same unit of work?"
- Asked by **engineering teams maintaining a service**. Ranked first.
- Acts by deciding whether to ship, roll back, or keep optimizing.
- Needs telemetry attributable to a stable software entity, aligned to a defined observation window, with energy and activity reported over that same period and the calculation method and provenance stated alongside the number.

### 2nd "Which option delivers equivalent work for less impact?"
- Asked by **teams choosing between vendors, hardware, regions, or service tiers**, including procurement functions comparing suppliers in a tender and finance functions weighing cost against footprint. Ranked second.
- Acts by selecting a provider, or migrating to one.
- Needs not the fine-grained attribution the first case demands, but each figure to declare its boundary and method clearly enough that two numbers from different systems or providers are never read as equivalent when they are not, to state whether embodied emissions are included or reported apart, and to mark where the comparison relies on infrastructure the reporting party cannot directly observe.

### 3rd "Is our product's or fleet's footprint falling?"
- Asked by **organizational and sustainability reporting functions**. Ranked last.
- Acts by answering customer, investor, and regulatory questions.
- Accepts monthly or slower cadence, regional rather than local grid data, and aggregation across workloads that erases which release or component drove a change, provided the report still states its boundary, sources, and method.

### Where these conflict
- The engineering case prevails on granularity, and the comparison case prevails on what a figure must declare about itself.

### What falls outside
- These conventions describe how an observed or estimated value identifies itself, the entity, window, method, and boundary it carries, rather than how the value must be calculated or which instrument produces it.
